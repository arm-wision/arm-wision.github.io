\par\bigskip\textbf{Abstract.}
\fi
%
We address multi-label plant species classification in PlantCLEF 2026,
where the goal is to predict every species present in a vegetation-quadrat
image. The central challenge is a domain shift, with training images
showing single-plant specimens while test images show dense multi-species
plots, compounded by a long-tailed label distribution. Our system
partially fine-tunes BioCLIP~2.5 ViT-H/14, unfreezing only the last four
transformer blocks to preserve its pretrained representation, on an
enlarged manifest that combines PlantCLEF~2024 with a research-grade
iNaturalist subset (2.65~million single-plant images, 7{,}806 species)
using auxiliary genus- and family-level heads intended to regularise the
species head. At inference each quadrat is split into a $4{\times}4$ grid
of 16 tiles forwarded at $224$ and $336$ pixels, the per-tile softmax
distributions are averaged, class-prior logit adjustment is applied, and
an adaptive probability threshold selects the species set. Our best
public Macro F1 is \textbf{0.418} (\textbf{0.403} private). Project
website: \url{https://arm-wision.github.io/}.
