\chapter{Introduction}
\label{sec:introduction}

Vegetation surveys require expert botanists to identify every species within a standardised quadrat, a process that is accurate but slow and limited by the supply of trained taxonomists~\cite{lifeclef2026}. PlantCLEF 2026~\cite{plantclef2026, lifeclef2026} formulates this as an automated recognition task: given a high-resolution photograph of a quadrat, a system predicts the set of species present from a taxonomy of roughly 7,800 candidates. Submissions are scored by the Macro F1, the per-quadrat F1 over the predicted species set averaged first within and then across transects so that densely sampled sites do not dominate,
\[
  \frac{1}{N}\sum_{i=1}^{N}\frac{1}{T_i}\sum_{j=1}^{T_i}\mathrm{F1}_{j},
\]
where $N$ is the number of transects, $T_i$ the number of quadrats in transect $i$, and $\mathrm{F1}_{j}$ the F1 between the predicted and true species sets for quadrat $j$.

The benchmark is difficult for two reasons. The training images show single specimens with one label each, whereas the test images are dense vegetation scenes in which many species occlude one another, so a model trained on isolated specimens transfers poorly to them. The label distribution is also heavily long-tailed, which biases standard cross-entropy training toward common species and depresses the Macro F1.

This report describes our submission, entered as ARM Wision, which placed seventh on the official leaderboard. We partially fine-tune BioCLIP~2.5~\cite{gu2025bioclip2}, a contrastive vision-language model whose pretraining on TreeOfLife-200M aligns its feature space with the Linnaean taxonomy, unfreezing only the last four transformer blocks of the ViT-H/14 backbone to preserve its pretrained representation and adding auxiliary genus- and family-level heads intended to regularise the encoder. We train on an enlarged manifest of 2.65 million labelled images. At inference each quadrat is split into a $4{\times}4$ grid of 16 tiles, encoded at $224$ and $336$ pixels and averaged, and class-prior logit adjustment with an adaptive probability threshold selects the output set. The system reaches a public Macro F1 of 0.418.
