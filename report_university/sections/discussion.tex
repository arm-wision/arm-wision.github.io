\section{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

The analysis in Section~\ref{sec:analysis} points to one practical conclusion: real headroom on this benchmark requires structurally stronger visual models, such as end to end fine tuning that preserves the pretraining prior, multi crop test time augmentation, or synthetic mosaic training distributions, rather than post hoc reweighting with auxiliary priors. At the same time, several limitations qualify what our results establish.

Our strongest model, i002, differs from the earlier 010 fine tune along several axes at once (training data, schedule, head topology, and head count), so we report i002 as the stronger configuration without isolating a single cause. Nor did we train a species only control without the auxiliary heads, so the auxiliary losses are reported as a deliberate regulariser rather than a measured improvement. The factors we did isolate are the number of unfrozen blocks, with a sharp optimum at four (Section~\ref{sec:blocks_ablation}), and the i003 species cap (Section~\ref{sec:longtail}), where only the manifest changed and the score dropped. Our exploration as a whole is a development trace in which most comparisons change several factors at once rather than a controlled ablation.

The public and private leaderboards also rank our configurations differently: our best public submission ($0.41826$) is not our best private one ($0.40600$, from a logit adjusted single resolution run). Because most pivot deltas lie within the run to run noise floor of roughly $0.002$ (Section~\ref{sec:eval_protocol}), we carry both columns throughout and avoid implying that the best public run is best overall.

Several directions for future work follow from these findings. A synthetic mosaic training regime that composes single plant images into multi species scenes with controlled overlap would let the network see the quadrat distribution during training, which no available corpus provides at scale. A location conditioned bioclimatic prior from training image coordinates and WorldClim variables could add evidence the image cannot supply, and is blocked here only by the absence of test site coordinates. Finally, the calibration parameters and the $4{\times}4$ tiling were fixed pragmatically under a limited submission budget; per class threshold calibration and a systematic grid and tile size sweep on a held out multi species split would establish whether this operating point is optimal or merely adequate.
