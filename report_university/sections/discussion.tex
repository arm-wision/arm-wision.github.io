\chapter{Discussion and Limitations}
\label{sec:discussion}

The analysis in Section~\ref{sec:analysis} indicates that further gains require structurally stronger visual models, for example end-to-end fine-tuning that preserves the pretrained representation or synthetic mosaic training that exposes the model to multi-species scenes. Post-hoc reweighting with auxiliary priors did not help. Several limitations qualify what our results establish.

Our strongest model, i002, differs from the earlier 010 fine-tune along several axes at once (training data, schedule, head topology, and head count), so we report i002 as the stronger configuration without isolating a single cause. We also did not train a species-only control with the auxiliary heads removed, so we report the auxiliary losses as a regulariser whose benefit we did not measure directly; the regularisation claim should be read as a hypothesis rather than a result. The factors we did isolate are the number of unfrozen blocks, with a sharp optimum at four (Section~\ref{sec:blocks_ablation}), and the i003 manifest change (Section~\ref{sec:longtail}), which combined tail augmentation with head capping and lowered the score. Our exploration as a whole is a development trace; most comparisons change several factors at once and are therefore not controlled ablations.

The public and private leaderboards also rank our configurations differently: our best public submission ($0.418$) is not our best private one ($0.406$, from a logit-adjusted single-resolution run). Because most variant deltas lie within the run-to-run noise floor of roughly $0.002$ (Section~\ref{sec:eval_protocol}), we carry both columns throughout and avoid implying that the best public run is best overall.

Several directions for future work follow from these findings. A synthetic mosaic training regime that composes single-plant images into multi-species scenes with controlled overlap would let the network see the quadrat distribution during training, which no available corpus provides at scale. A location-conditioned bioclimatic prior from training-image coordinates and WorldClim variables could add evidence the image cannot supply, and is blocked here only by the absence of test-site coordinates. Finally, the calibration parameters and the $4{\times}4$ tiling were fixed pragmatically under a limited submission budget; per-class threshold calibration and a systematic grid- and tile-size sweep on a held-out multi-species split would establish whether this operating point is optimal or merely adequate.
